About the book

From frozen to alive

I grew up in a home where words were sharper than a knife and silence was the only shelter. For years I lived in survival mode. I functioned, I smiled, I did what was expected of me. But I didn’t feel.

This book is the story of my journey. I write about what happens to a child who has to switch off her emotions in order to survive, and about how a grown woman can get them back. Honestly, without embellishment, and with tenderness for every woman who recognises herself in these pages.

This is not a guide to “fixing yourself” in a week. It’s a way back to who you truly are.

I will guide you through ACoA and complex trauma, through the legacy of your ancestors and Family Constellations, to working with your inner child, your body and your frozen emotions.

Chapter 1

My Story: From Surviving to Living

Every story of healing begins in the place that hurt. In the moment when something inside us broke — or rather, kept breaking for years, so slowly that we never noticed when we stopped feeling. We stopped dreaming. We stopped being ourselves.

I, too, once could not feel.

I didn’t know how.

I grew up in a world that was not safe. In a home where words were sharper than a knife, and my mother’s silence hurt more than shouting. Where you had to be a grown-up before you had learned to write. Where being a child was a luxury I could not afford.

My body lived, functioned, carried out its duties — but my soul was locked inside a shell. Hard, cold, armoured. That was how I learned to survive.

About the author

Joanna Dorobisz

She grew up in a home where violence was part of everyday life and silence was the only protection. Through Family Constellations, following Bert Hellinger’s method, and deep inner work, she broke the cycle and found her voice.

Today she is an author, a Family Constellations facilitator and the founder of Let Your Soul Glow. She helps women reclaim their voice, their feelings and the right to their own lives.
