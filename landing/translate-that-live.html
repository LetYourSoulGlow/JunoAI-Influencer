Sound familiar?

It's past midnight, and the text just says "fine."

You've read it nine times. You've screenshotted it to a friend, who said "he's just busy", which is what friends say, lovingly, without reading the text properly. You've typed a reply, deleted it, typed a softer one, and deleted that too.

Text strips out the voice, the face and the pause before the sentence. Your brain fills the gap with whatever mood it's already in. When you're anxious, it picks the worst one.

Most texts that feel like a verdict are really a mood, a habit, or a typo with feelings.

Translate That gives you a calm second reading for the moments when you can't find one yourself, and the exact words to send next.

Card 01 · Part 1 · They sent something short

He sent "fine."

Fine.

What they might have meant

  • Tired, and "fine" is the shortest sentence available.
  • Hurt, and hoping you will ask.
  • Actually fine. It happens.

How to tell which

Look at what you sent right before it. A cancelled plan, a late reply or a joke that landed badly points to hurt. A meme, or a question about Thursday, points to tired. Check the clock too: "fine." at 11pm after a long day is rarely a message about you.

Don't send

"Okay, good."  /  "Why are you being like this?"
Both answer the full stop and ignore the person.

Send it kinder

If they sound tiredSounds like a long day. No reply needed. I'm here when you've got words again.
If they sound hurtI think I missed something. Can we talk tonight? No pressure, I just want to get it right.
If you can't tellFine as in fine, or fine as in 'ask me again'? Happy to do either.
Juno's note. I have read a great many "fine."s. It is the most overloaded word in English, and it carries its full stop like a briefcase. Don't open the briefcase by force. Knock.

The method, in four rules

Small enough to remember at 2am

The Three Layers

There's what they wrote, what they might have meant, and what you're afraid it means. Read them in that order. Fear goes last.

Ask Beats Guess

If you can't tell which meaning is true, ask one light question. Every guess is a small gamble with someone else's feelings.

The Second Text Rule

After you send, you don't send another until they've replied, or until tomorrow morning. One text. Then wait.

Knock, Don't Open the Briefcase

A short, closed text has something inside it. Knock once, kindly. Don't force the lock.

Is it only for dating?

No. Most cards work just as well with friends, family and colleagues. A friend's "fine." is as loaded as a partner's. The cards say "they" for everyone, so they fit any gender and any pairing.

Is Juno a real person?

No. Juno Vale is an AI character created by Let Your Soul Glow. The book says so on the first page, and we'd rather you knew before you buy.

Will this help me win someone back?

It will help you say what you mean, kindly and clearly. It won't make anyone feel something they don't, and it contains nothing designed to manipulate. If a line only works by tricking someone, it isn't in this book.
