# Infographic 02 — Never send these at 2am

Source: bonus "Never Send at 2am" from Juno's book *Translate That*
("Ten messages that feel brilliant at night and read very differently at 9am, plus one safe fallback").
The five messages below are written in Juno's voice; compare with the PDF and swap if the book has better ones.

Format: 9:16, 2×3 grid, night mood (deep teal background, cream cards). Juno = Element `juno-vale1`.

## Copy

- Title: **Never send these at 2am**
- Subtitle: *Brilliant at night. Very different at 9am.*

| # | 2am 🌙 | 9am ☀️ | Illustration (moving detail) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Are you awake?" | Lonely, not curious. | phone on pillow, moon + drifting clouds |
| 2 | "I'm not mad, I'm just saying..." | A paragraph isn't a conversation. | endless message scrolling |
| 3 | "Do you ever think about us?" | Nostalgia isn't a plan. | two mugs, candle flickering |
| 4 | "Why did you like her photo?" | Nobody wins a 2am investigation. | magnifying glass over glowing phone |
| 5 | "Fine. Forget it." | Ends the chat, starts the fight. | wall clock at 2:00, second hand ticking |
| 6 | Juno — "The safe fallback:" **Can we talk tomorrow?** | | Juno presses phone to chest |
