# Infographic 01 — Green flags that might be red flags

Format: vertical 9:16 (1080×1920), for Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts.
Brand: Juno Vale · "Send it kinder." · teal #2c5b5d, coral #e2725b, cream #fbf4e8, ink #1f2e2f.
Tone: Juno doesn't hand out verdicts. One moment is a mood; a pattern is a flag.
Gender-neutral "they", like in Juno's books.

## Copy (on-screen text)

- Title: **Green flags that might be red flags**
- Subtitle: *Sweet once. Worrying as a pattern.*

| # | Looks like 💚 | Ask yourself 🚩 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | They text you all day | Checking in, or checking up? |
| 2 | "You're the one" after a week | Fast isn't the same as deep. |
| 3 | All their exes were "crazy" | Who is never at fault? |
| 4 | Jealous "because they care" | Care asks. Control demands. |
| 5 | They want every free minute | Do you still see your friends? |

- Closing line: **One moment is a mood. A pattern is a flag.**
- Signature: *— Juno · Send it kinder.*
