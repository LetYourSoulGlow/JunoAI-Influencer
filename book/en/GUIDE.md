# Translation guide: "Cisza, z której pochodzę" → "The Silence I Come From"

Author: Joanna Dorobisz, a Family Constellations facilitator (Bert Hellinger method). Self-help/memoir hybrid for women healing from a dysfunctional/alcoholic family home, C-PTSD, generational trauma.

Style
- British English spelling (colour, recognise, behaviour), natural literary English, warm, intimate, direct. Not a word-for-word calque: rephrase so it reads as if written in English, but keep every idea, image, and paragraph. Do not add, cut, summarise or soften content.
- Keep the author's rhythm: short one-line paragraphs stay short; repetitions used for effect stay.
- The reader is addressed as "you" (Polish capitalised "Ty/Cię/Twój" → plain lowercase "you/your").
- Use em dashes with spaces " — " as in the source. Use curly quotes “ ” and ’.
- Bullet lines start with "• " — keep that.

Fixed terms
- Ustawienia Systemowe / ustawienia hellingerowskie → Family Constellations
- ustawienie (session) → constellation; ustawiać → set up a constellation
- system rodzinny / ród → family system / family line; przodkowie → ancestors
- porządki miłości → the Orders of Love; uwikłanie → entanglement; lojalność → loyalty
- pole (wiedzące) → the (knowing) field; zastępca/reprezentant → representative
- DDA (Dorosłe Dzieci Alkoholików) → ACoA (Adult Children of Alcoholics); DDD → adult children of dysfunctional families
- c-PTSD → C-PTSD; wewnętrzne dziecko → the inner child
- Ćwiczenia i pytania refleksyjne → Exercises and Reflective Questions
- Headers: "Rozdział N: X" → "Chapter N: X"; "Zakończenie: X" → "Conclusion: X"; "Epilog" → "Epilogue"
- Polish helplines/institutions: keep the name, add a short English gloss in brackets if needed.
